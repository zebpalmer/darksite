# Intro

The opening of an **overview page** — a product's home, a section's start page: a title, a sentence
or two on what it is, and up to two actions. It takes the place of the `PageHeader` there.

Not a marketing hero: no image, no color band, no full-bleed background. The title is 24px on a
narrow page and 32px from 560px of the intro's own width; the lead is 16px `ds-text-2` at a
readable measure.

## What the consumer provides

`title`, `lead` (the sentence or two), `level` (heading level, default 1), `actions` snippet (a
primary and a secondary `Button`), children (anything after the lead), `align` (`start` default |
`center`), `class`.

## Don't

- Don't put it on a work or list page. It pushes the work below the fold.
- Don't write more than two sentences; link to a document page for the rest.
- Don't give it more than two actions.
