# Notices

darksite's prebuilt brands `nord`, `dracula`, `solarized`, `gruvbox`, `catppuccin`, `tokyo-night`,
`rose-pine` and `one-dark` take their colors from these open-source color schemes. darksite fills
in the modes a scheme does not define and adjusts colors that miss its contrast targets, so the
brands are adaptations, not the schemes themselves. The names belong to their projects.

| `nord` | Nord | https://github.com/nordtheme/nord | MIT |
| `dracula` | Dracula | https://github.com/dracula/dracula-theme | MIT |
| `solarized` | Solarized | https://github.com/altercation/solarized | MIT |
| `gruvbox` | Gruvbox | https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox | MIT/X11 (stated in its README) |
